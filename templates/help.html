Help

What is Ryokan?

Ryokan is a self-hosted anime PVR (Personal Video Recorder). It searches Nyaa for anime releases, scores them based on your preferences, and sends the best matches to your configured torrent client (qBittorrent, Deluge, Transmission, or rTorrent) for downloading. It uses AniList as its metadata source instead of TVDB, which means it understands how anime is actually released and catalogued.

Getting Started

After creating your account, head to Settings → Integrations and configure your download client (qBittorrent, Deluge, Transmission, or rTorrent). Enter the URL, credentials, and a scoping label or category. Then go to Search to find releases on Nyaa. Results are scored automatically based on your configured preferences.

Search Tips

Use the uploader field to filter by a specific release group's Nyaa account. The category filter lets you narrow to English-translated or raw releases. "Trusted only" shows releases from verified Nyaa uploaders.

Try searching for batch releases by including "batch" in your query, e.g. "Jujutsu Kaisen batch 1080p".

Grabbing Releases

Click the Grab button next to any result to send it to your download client. The torrent will be added with your configured scoping label (or category on qBittorrent). Make sure the client is running and the connection is configured in Settings.

Forgot your password?

Recovery steps live on the forgot-password page, which is also reachable from the login screen when you're signed out.
